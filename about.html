About Medora Health

Fifteen years of care,
countless lives changed.

From a single clinic in downtown New York to a nationally recognized healthcare network — this is the story of Medora Health.

Who We Are

A hospital built around the patient, not the paperwork

Medora Health was founded in 2011 by three physicians who were tired of watching healthcare become a conveyor belt. Today we operate 8 facilities across the Northeast, employ over 600 clinicians and staff, and serve more than 50,000 patients a year — yet every appointment still begins the same way: with a conversation.

We combine board-certified specialists, AI-assisted diagnostics, and genuinely warm care to deliver outcomes that consistently rank in the top 5% of the region.
