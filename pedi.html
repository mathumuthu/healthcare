A Legacy of Healing, Built on Trust

For over two decades, Starkly Healthcare has stood beside the families of Salem — growing from a single clinic into a full-fledged multi-specialty hospital.

Who We Are

Company Overview

Starkly Healthcare is a 350-bed multi-specialty hospital located in the heart of Salem, Tamil Nadu. We bring together 120+ specialist doctors, advanced surgical suites, NABL-grade laboratories and a 24/7 emergency department — all guided by one belief: medicine works best when it is human.

Every year, more than 60,000 patients trust us with their care — from routine check-ups to life-saving critical interventions.

  • 350-bed tertiary care facility
  • 50+ medical and surgical specialities
  • Fully digital, paperless patient records
  • Dedicated international patient desk

Our Journey

The Starkly Story

1999

A Clinic is Born

Dr. K. Saravanan opens a 10-bed clinic in Chinna Tirupathi with three staff and a promise: treat every patient like family.

2006

First Specialty Wings

Cardiology and Orthopedics departments open, along with the region’s first 24/7 cath lab.

2014

Multi-Specialty Expansion

The hospital grows to 200 beds, adding Neurology, Oncology and a dedicated pediatric intensive care unit.

2020

Digital Transformation

Fully paperless records, telemedicine consultations and robotic-assisted surgery arrive at Starkly.

2026

Today

350 beds, 120+ doctors, 3 lakh patients healed — and the same founding promise, kept every day.
